Ken's Labyrinth 2.01 (Full Version) - licences of the files on this disk

This index was compiled for the unemu media library from the files of
LABFULL.ZIP, as Ken Silverman distributes it on his official Ken's Labyrinth
page, https://advsys.net/ken/klab.htm. It is not a document of the author;
the index itself is dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. The disk
holds every file of LABFULL.ZIP, unmodified, under its own name and in the
archive's order; no file of the archive is left out. The game displays its
version as V2.01; the author's page names the archive v2.1.

The terms: LicenseRef-KensLabyrinth-Freeware
  README.TXT, which this set also carries as its licence text. On 16
  November 1999 Ken Silverman, "with permission from Epic MegaGames",
  released the full version of Ken's Labyrinth for free: "You may copy this
  software freely." It asks two things of anyone who copies it:
    1) "This software can not be sold for profit in any way."
    2) "When distributing, please do not remove any files that came with
       this original archive. This includes all text files."
  It also says to ignore the order information, that many of the phone
  numbers and addresses in the other files are out of date, and that the
  author gives no support. The author's page lists LABFULL.ZIP as the
  freeware full version and says it "now includes a fix (KENSBFIX.EXE) for
  the 'Digitized Sound' crash". The notice "THIS IS NOT SHAREWARE / DO NOT
  DISTRIBUTE" in the game's graphics predates the 1999 freeware release,
  which supersedes it.

Contributors
  The credits (HELPME.DOC) name others besides the author: artwork by
  Future Crew (the game's ART tile names Mikko Iho), level design and
  artwork by Andy Cotter, who also wrote the hint manual (HINTS.TXT), and
  the voice of Sparky by Mark Rein. Their parts are covered only by the
  author's 1999 release, made with Epic MegaGames' permission. KEN.EXE,
  KENSBFIX.EXE and SETUP.EXE contain the Microsoft C run-time library ("MS
  Run-Time Library - Copyright (c) 1990, Microsoft Corp"), as the compiler
  linked it.
