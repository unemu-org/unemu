Classic light — readable progress, fitting dialogs, visible focus

Design proposal for review. Each pair is Now / Proposed at 1 CSS px per UI point (100% zoom). The viewport frames are exactly 760 × 500 px; scroll the comparison horizontally on a narrow display. No emulator wiring.

The 12 px Tahoma/Verdana font stack and bevels follow the existing mock-ups. Exact egui glyph metrics and conditional text wrapping are not reproduced. Sizes below are source-derived layout estimates, not measurements from a running window.

1. Push progress text

Recommend the existing blob/byte status as normal text above the bar: it stays readable at every fill level and does not depend on selection text colour. The status line costs 22 px, included in the proposed dialog body budget. A single text colour inside the bar would need to contrast with both white and navy; moving the text also accommodates longer status messages.

2. Fit the minimum 760 × 500 px window

Now frames illustrate the reported ~538 px revision and ~516 px Push planning cases. Content, warnings, checks and line wrapping can make either taller. Proposed sizes include the frame and title strip; buttons stay outside the scrollable body. Scroll inside each proposed body to inspect its remaining content.

Recommend scrolling the whole revision body, including the name form, notices and review, with Publish revision / Back / Cancel pinned below it. Replace the fixed 330 px review reservation and nested previews with one body scroll area. At 760 × 500 the outer dialog is 694 × 476 px, with a 389 px body; long warnings and errors use that body too. Tighter spacing alone would leave conditional notices able to push the buttons off-screen.

Recommend the same bounded body for every Push stage, keeping the verdict and all checks available by scrolling, with Push / Cancel pinned. At 760 × 500 the outer dialog is 726 × 476 px and the body is 389 px. The running stage uses the separate status line from proposal 1 inside that budget. Local chooser, dry-run and blob-list height caps do not constrain the sum of the dialog; reducing only one cap would remain content-dependent.

Recommend capping the outer wizard at viewport minus 24 px on both axes, reducing the step column to 160 px, and letting the page take the remaining 550 px rather than forcing 620 px. At 760 × 500 it is 736 × 476 px, with a 389 px body and pinned navigation. Reflow long field rows and wrap explanations within the page; preserve all seven steps. The existing height formula already gives a 310 px body at this window size, so the primary defect is forced width. Scroll the body for taller pages rather than shrinking controls.

3. Focused single-line field — visible outline decided

Recommend a solid 1 px inset outline in the existing selection navy, #22468a, around the focused single-line field. It is familiar in the classic palette and contrasts about 9.1:1 with the white field. Keep white selected glyphs on navy; the focus boundary must use its own paint colour. The inspected shell already has outline_focused_field (crates/window/src/shell.rs:6378–6403), which repaints focus using the field’s text colour; the left sample documents the underlying egui defect, while the navy sample proposes the explicit classic light treatment for approval.
